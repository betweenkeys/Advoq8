    Pick what they might say back, and plan your reply.

Two costs to weigh

If you say it while you're angry

It can come out as blame. Then the talk turns to your tone, not your need.

If you swallow it

The resentment stays. Next time will likely look the same.

Feeling frustrated is not the problem. You get to choose how you respond.
